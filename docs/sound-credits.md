# Sound credits

The four book sounds on the favourites shelf (`js/sounds.js`, played by `js/bookopen.js`) are short cuts from public-domain recordings on Wikimedia Commons. They came from pdsounds.org and are marked Public domain there. Each cut has a short fade in and out and its level adjusted; nothing else is changed.

| Sound | Used for | Recording | Recordist | Licence | Cut |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| pull | the book sliding off the shelf | [Book paper pages assorted.ogg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Book_paper_pages_assorted.ogg) | stephan | Public domain | 70.42–70.82 s |
| open | the cover turning open | [Turning a page.ogg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Turning_a_page.ogg) | planish | Public domain | 1.50–2.42 s |
| shut | the book closing | [Old book.ogg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Old_book.ogg) | cori | Public domain | 62.78–63.25 s |
| push | the book going back on the shelf | [Book paper pages assorted.ogg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Book_paper_pages_assorted.ogg) then [Old book.ogg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Old_book.ogg) | stephan; cori | Public domain | 72.83–73.15 s (slow fade in and out, low-passed at 5 kHz), then 58.12–58.40 s (much quieter, low-passed at 500 Hz, so only a soft muffled knock); about 9 dB quieter than the pull (changed 2026-11-07: the first version's knock was too loud) |

Checked 2026-11-07. If a clip cannot be decoded, `js/bookopen.js` falls back to sounds made in the browser with Web Audio.
