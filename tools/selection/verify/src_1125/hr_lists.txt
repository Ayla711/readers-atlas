# A2: Jutarnji list poll (2010; Booksa, 10 Jan 2011, calls it "last week's"), about 40 (en.wikipedia: 41) scholars, writers and public figures asked for the best Croatian novel. Only the result for the winner is documented: Kiklop (hr.wikipedia "Kiklop (roman)", en.wikipedia "Ranko Marinković"). Booksa (booksa.hr/vijesti/sve/o-radostima-cenzure) says the choices were mostly Kiklop, Povratak Filipa Latinovicza (sometimes Na rubu pameti), Andrić's novels and, fairly often, Proljeća Ivana Galeba, but gives no ranking; the Jutarnji article itself was not found. So A2 is counted for Kiklop only.
